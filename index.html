Our Story

Khaddar Heritage & Craftsmanship

“In the middle of Rajjar Bazaar, cloth you can hold in your hands — and a name Charsadda trusts.”

Shah Khaddar Handicraft, Wholesale Dealer, stands at Shop No. 1, Haji Wareed Khan Market in Rajjar Main Bazaar — the busy shopping heart of Charsadda. Proprietors Haji Shah Saud and Shah Hussain welcome wholesale and retail customers alike, with stacks of winter khaddar, walls of shawls and chaddars, and fabrics for the whole family under one roof.

Families from across Charsadda and nearby areas come to Rajjar Main Bazaar for their seasonal shopping, and our shop is part of that daily bazaar life — honest dealing, cloth you can feel before you buy, and help choosing colours for Eid, weddings and everyday wear.
